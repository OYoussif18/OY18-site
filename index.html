02Basis

How I
approach it

I am drawn to technology from the outside in: the point where a system meets the person using it, or the person trying to break it.

  1. A

    Read the mechanism

    Skip the summary. Find the part that actually decides the behaviour.

  2. B

    Tear it down

    Assume the boundary is wrong somewhere, then go and find where.

  3. C

    Rebuild it small

    Small enough to understand completely. That is the point of the exercise.

  4. D

    Put it in a loop

    Hand it to an agent, a script, or a person. Watch where it breaks.

I am still early in most of this. The honest version is that I am building the understanding and the artefacts at the same time.
